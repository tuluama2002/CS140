by Tag, Class, ID

What is the difference between a SPAN tag and a DIV tag

Span tag takes as much space it needs and stays on the same line, it also used to style a small part of the text. Div tag takes the whole line and always starts on a new line, it also used to group big sections

What is the difference between a DIV tag and a HEADER, MAIN, or FOOTER?
they all have the same purpose but DIV tag has no meaning, google cannot recognize it, like header, google sees everything within that tag as header
