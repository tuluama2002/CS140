Questions

What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags start on a new line and stretch across the full width of the page, like p, div, and h1. Inline tags sit inside a line of text and are only as wide as their content, like strong, em, and a.

What is the margin, border, and padding?

Padding is the space between the content and the border. The border is the line around the padding. The margin is the empty space outside the border that pushes other boxes away.

What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?

Absolute units like px, and relative units like em, rem, %, vw, and vh. Relative units change with the font size or the size of the window, while px stays the same.

When would you use a figure tag?

Use a figure when an image, chart, or code sample goes with a caption and should stand on its own as one block, separate from the surrounding text.
